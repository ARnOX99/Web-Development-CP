Severe infestation reported in maize fields across Karnataka and Telangana. Spray Spinetoram 11.7% SC @ 0.5 ml/litre immediately.

Karnataka, Telangana, AP

💡 Best Farming Practices

Irrigation Tips

  • Use drip or sprinkler irrigation to save up to 40% water
  • Irrigate during early morning or evening to reduce evaporation
  • Avoid waterlogging — it damages root systems
  • Mulching reduces soil moisture loss significantly

Fertiliser Tips

  • Always get a Soil Health Card before applying fertilisers
  • Apply nitrogen in split doses for better absorption
  • Use bio-fertilisers (Rhizobium, PSB) to reduce chemical input
  • Avoid excess urea — it degrades soil health long term

Soil Health Tips

  • Practice crop rotation every season to maintain nutrients
  • Add organic manure / FYM at least once per year
  • Avoid burning crop residue — incorporate it into the soil
  • Green manuring with Dhaincha or Sunhemp improves fertility

Pest Management Tips

  • Follow Integrated Pest Management (IPM) practices
  • Use sticky yellow traps for early pest detection
  • Spray pesticides only when pest crosses economic threshold
  • Wear protective gear while spraying chemicals
