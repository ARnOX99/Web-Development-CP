About Krishi Seva Portal

The Krishi Seva Portal is an initiative by the Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare, Government of India. Our mission is to digitally connect every farmer with government resources, schemes, and real-time agricultural guidance — directly from their phone or computer.

We aim to reduce the information gap between the government and farmers by providing a single, easy-to-use platform available in English and regional languages.
