Government Agricultural Schemes

Explore all active central government schemes designed to support and empower Indian farmers

Financial Aid

PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi provides ₹6,000 per year in three equal instalments to all landholding farmer families across India.

  • Benefit: ₹6,000/year
  • Eligibility: All landholding farmers
  • Mode: Direct Bank Transfer
Apply Now
Crop Insurance

PMFBY

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana offers financial support to farmers suffering crop loss or damage due to unforeseen events like floods or drought.

  • Premium: 2% for Kharif, 1.5% for Rabi
  • Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops
  • Mode: Via Banks / CSCs
Apply Now
Credit

Kisan Credit Card

The KCC scheme provides farmers with timely and adequate credit for agriculture and allied activities at subsidised interest rates.

  • Benefit: Credit up to ₹3 Lakh @ 4% p.a.
  • Eligibility: All farmers, SHGs, JLGs
  • Mode: Via Nationalised Banks
Apply Now
Soil Management

Soil Health Card

This scheme provides every farmer a Soil Health Card with crop-wise recommendations of nutrients and fertilisers for individual farm plots.

  • Benefit: Free soil testing & card
  • Eligibility: All farmers
  • Mode: Via Krishi Kendra
Apply Now
Irrigation

PM Krishi Sinchai Yojana

Aims to ensure access to some means of protective irrigation to all agricultural farms and improve water use efficiency with "More Crop Per Drop".

  • Benefit: Drip & sprinkler subsidy up to 90%
  • Eligibility: All farmers with land records
  • Mode: State Agriculture Dept.
Apply Now
Food Security

National Food Security Mission

NFSM aims to increase production of rice, wheat, pulses, and coarse cereals through area expansion and productivity enhancement.

  • Benefit: Free seeds, tools & training
  • Eligibility: Small & marginal farmers
  • Mode: Via District Agriculture Office
Apply Now
Organic Farming

Paramparagat Krishi Vikas Yojana

PKVY promotes organic farming through adoption of organic village by cluster approach and certification. Helps farmers get premium prices for organic produce.

  • Benefit: ₹50,000/hectare over 3 years
  • Eligibility: Farmer groups (min. 20 farmers)
  • Mode: Via State Agriculture Dept.
Apply Now
Irrigation

Micro Irrigation Fund

NABARD manages a dedicated Micro Irrigation Fund of ₹5,000 crore to expand coverage of micro irrigation beyond the provisions under PMKSY.

  • Benefit: Low interest loans for drip/sprinkler
  • Eligibility: State Govts & Farmers
  • Mode: Via NABARD / State Nodal Agency
Apply Now
